# 10: Cashflow Sankey

**What to build:** The Cashflow tab shows how money moved through the Household or an Account over the selected period, as a Sankey diagram.

**Blocked by:** 04, 05

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Sources: Deposits, Payouts, Interest, Coupons and Trade sale proceeds.
- [ ] Destinations: Trade purchases by asset class, Withdrawals, Withheld Tax and remaining cash.
- [ ] The flows balance: sources equal destinations.
- [ ] The Household view leaves out Internal Transfers.
- [ ] Domain-core tests check the flow totals and that they balance.
